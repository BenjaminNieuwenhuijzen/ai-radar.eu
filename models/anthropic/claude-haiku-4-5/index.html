Claude Haiku 4.5

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Claude Haiku 4.5 is a language model in Anthropic's Haiku line of small, fast models that accepts text and images, with a 200K-token context window and an extended thinking mode. It is offered in the Claude apps, Claude Code, on the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Anthropic presents it as a drop-in replacement for Claude 3.5 Haiku and Claude Sonnet 4.
