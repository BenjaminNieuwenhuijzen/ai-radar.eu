Claude Haiku 5.5

Announced · Language

Claude Haiku 5.5 is a forthcoming model in Anthropic's Claude Haiku line, first named in the Claude Opus 5.5 announcement of 22 September 2026 as following within weeks. Anthropic says it is aimed at high-volume, cost-sensitive applications; on 1 October 2026 the models overview still listed Claude Haiku 4.5 as the current Haiku model. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
