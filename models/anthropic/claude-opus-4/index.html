Claude Opus 4

Retired · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Claude Opus 4 is a language model in Anthropic's Opus line that accepts text and images, released together with Claude Sonnet 4 and offered in paid Claude plans, on the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud Vertex AI. Anthropic describes it as a hybrid model with near-instant answers and an extended thinking mode, and released it under its ASL-3 Standard as a precaution.
