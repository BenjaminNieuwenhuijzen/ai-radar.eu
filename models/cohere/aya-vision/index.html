Aya Vision

Available · Language, Multimodal

Aya Vision is a multilingual vision-language model from Cohere For AI (now Cohere Labs) that takes text and images and produces text in 23 languages, released in 8B and 32B sizes as open weights, on the Cohere platform and through WhatsApp. The 32B model is built on Aya Expanse 32B, the 8B model on Command R7B.
