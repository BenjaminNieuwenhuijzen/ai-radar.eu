Command R 08-2024

Available · Language

Command R 08-2024 is Cohere's August 2024 update of Command R, a text-only language model with a 128k-token context, released on 30 August 2024 on the Cohere API, Amazon SageMaker and Oracle OCI and on Microsoft Azure from September. Cohere reports higher throughput, lower latency and lower prices, and added Safety Modes in beta; 32-billion-parameter research weights are on Hugging Face. [1] [2] [3] [4] ● Primary source
