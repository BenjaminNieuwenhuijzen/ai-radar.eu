Cohere models

Cohere is an AI company founded in Toronto in 2019 that builds foundation models and AI products for businesses. It offers its Command text-generation models and its Embed and Rerank models on its own platform and through cloud providers, and launched the North workplace platform in 2025. Its research lab, Cohere Labs, develops the open-weight Aya multilingual models.
