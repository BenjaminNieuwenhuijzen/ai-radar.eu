DeepSeek-V3.1

Available · Language, Reasoning

DeepSeek-V3.1 is a hybrid language model in DeepSeek's V line that offers a thinking and a non-thinking mode in one model, launched on 21 August 2025 in DeepSeek's web chat and API. Its weights and those of DeepSeek-V3.1-Base were published under the MIT License; DeepSeek describes the release as its first step toward the agent era. [1] [2]Introduction; License ● Primary source
