DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash is a Mixture-of-Experts model with 552B backbone parameters that takes text and images and generates text, released on 2026-09-10 in the DeepSeek API as deepseek-flash and with open weights under the MIT License. DeepSeek calls it the smallest model of a new architecture family and says it was trained from scratch. [1]Introduction; License [2] ● Primary source
