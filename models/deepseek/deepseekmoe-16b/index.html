DeepSeekMoE 16B

Available · Language

DeepSeekMoE 16B is an open-weights mixture-of-experts language model from DeepSeek with 16.4B total and about 2.8B activated parameters, released as a Base and a Chat checkpoint with the DeepSeekMoE research paper. Its architecture combines fine-grained expert segmentation with shared experts that are always active. [1]1. Introduction; 3. Model Downloads [2]section 5.1.2, Model Settings ● Primary source
