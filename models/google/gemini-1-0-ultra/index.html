Gemini 1.0 Ultra

Status unknown · Language, Multimodal · Milestone

Gemini 1.0 Ultra is the biggest of the three sizes of Google's Gemini 1.0 models, which Google describes as natively multimodal, announced in December 2023. It reached consumers as Ultra 1.0 in Gemini Advanced, part of the Google One AI Premium plan, in February 2024, and developers and Cloud customers a week later through the Gemini API and, via an allowlist, Vertex AI.
