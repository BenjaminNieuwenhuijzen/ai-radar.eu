Gemini-1.5-Flash-002

Retired · Language, Multimodal

Gemini-1.5-Flash-002 is an updated, production-ready version of Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash, released with Gemini-1.5-Pro-002 through Google AI Studio, the Gemini API and Vertex AI. Google says it builds on recent experimental releases; from November 2024 the gemini-1.5-flash model id in the Gemini API pointed to this version. [1] [2]November 21, 2024 ● Primary source
