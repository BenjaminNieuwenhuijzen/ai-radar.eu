Gemini 1.5 Flash-8B

Retired · Language, Multimodal

Gemini 1.5 Flash-8B is a smaller and faster variant of Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash, first released as an experimental API model in August 2024 and production-ready in Google AI Studio and the Gemini API from October 2024. Google positions it for simple, high-volume tasks; its technical report says the model is multimodal and supports a context window of over 1 million tokens.
