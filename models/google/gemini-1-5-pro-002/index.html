Gemini-1.5-Pro-002

Retired · Language, Multimodal

Gemini-1.5-Pro-002 is an updated, production-ready version of Google's Gemini 1.5 Pro, released with Gemini-1.5-Flash-002 through Google AI Studio, the Gemini API and Vertex AI, with a 2 million token context window. Google says it builds on recent experimental releases; a chat-optimized version came to Gemini Advanced in October 2024. [1] [2]2024.10.02 ● Primary source
