Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite

Retired · Language, Multimodal

Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite is a multimodal language model from Google, released in public preview on 5 February 2025 and generally available in the Gemini API, through Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, from 25 February. It took text, image, audio and video input with a context window of 1,048,576 tokens, returned text, and was shut down on 1 June 2026. [1] [2] [3]Model Information [4]June 1, 2026 ● Primary source
