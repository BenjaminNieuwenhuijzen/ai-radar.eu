Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking

Retired · Language, Reasoning

Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking is an experimental reasoning model from Google, built on Gemini 2.0 Flash and offered in preview in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio from December 2024 and in the Gemini app from February 2025. Google describes it as a test-time compute model that shows its thought process while generating a response; it never left experimental status. [1]December 19, 2024 [2] ● Primary source
