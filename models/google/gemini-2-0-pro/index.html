Gemini 2.0 Pro

Retired · Language, Multimodal

Gemini 2.0 Pro is a multimodal language model in Google's Gemini Pro line, released on 5 February 2025 only as an experimental model, in Google AI Studio, Vertex AI and the Gemini app for Gemini Advanced users. Google aimed it at coding and complex prompts; it had a 2 million token context window and could use Google Search and code execution as tools. [1] [2]footnote 1 ● Primary source
