Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite is a multimodal reasoning model in Google's Gemini Flash-Lite line, released in preview on 17 June 2025 and generally available in the Gemini API from 22 July 2025. It has a 1 million token context window, a thinking budget that can be set and is off by default, and tools such as grounding with Google Search and code execution.
