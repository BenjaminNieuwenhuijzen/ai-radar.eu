Gemini 2.5 Pro

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Gemini 2.5 Pro is a multimodal reasoning model in Google's Gemini Pro line, first released as an experimental model on 25 March 2025 and generally available from 17 June 2025 in the Gemini API and the Gemini app. Google describes it as a thinking model that reasons through its thoughts before responding; since 18 September 2026 API access is limited to earlier users.
