Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite is a multimodal language model and the first Flash-Lite model of Google's Gemini 3 series, released in preview on 3 March 2026 in the Gemini API and Vertex AI and generally available since 7 May 2026. Google aims it at high-volume workloads such as translation and content moderation, with thinking levels as standard. [1] [2]March 3, 2026; May 7, 2026 ● Primary source
