Gemini 3.5 Pro

Announced · Language

Gemini 3.5 Pro is an announced language model in Google's Gemini Pro line, named on 19 May 2026 as already in internal use, with a rollout planned for the following month. In July 2026 Google said it was testing the model with partners; it had not been released by 1 October 2026. [1]Introduction [2]introduction; Get started today [3] ● Primary source
