Gemini 3 Pro

Retired · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Gemini 3 Pro is a multimodal reasoning model in Google's Gemini Pro line and the first Gemini 3 model, released in preview on 18 November 2025 in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI and Google Antigravity. The model card describes a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer; the API preview was shut down on 9 March 2026. [1] [2]Model Information: Description [3]March 9, 2026 ● Primary source
