Gemini 4 Argon

Preview · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Gemini 4 Argon is a reasoning model in Google's Gemini line, announced on 30 September 2026 and first rolled out only to vetted cyber defenders in the Fairwind Program. Google describes it as built for sustained reasoning over long, multi-step work in software engineering, enterprise knowledge work and cybersecurity defense, with output of up to 1M tokens.
