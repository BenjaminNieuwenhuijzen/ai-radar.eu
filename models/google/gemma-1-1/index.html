Gemma 1.1

Available · Language

Gemma 1.1 is an update to Google's first-generation Gemma open models, released as instruction-tuned 2B and 7B checkpoints with downloadable weights under Google's Gemma terms of use. Google describes them as English-language, text-to-text, decoder-only language models, post-trained with a new RLHF method. [1] [2] ● Primary source
