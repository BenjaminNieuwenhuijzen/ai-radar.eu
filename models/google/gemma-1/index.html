Gemma

Available · Language · Milestone

Gemma is a family of lightweight open-weight language models from Google, released in February 2024 in 2B and 7B sizes, each pre-trained and instruction-tuned, and built from the research behind Gemini. The text-to-text, English-language models have an 8,192-token context window, and their terms of use allow responsible commercial use. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
