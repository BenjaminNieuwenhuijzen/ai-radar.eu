Gemma 2

Available · Language · Milestone

Gemma 2 is the second generation of Google's Gemma open-weight language models, released in 9B and 27B sizes with weights on Kaggle and Hugging Face, followed by a 2B size in July 2024. Google says it uses a redesigned architecture aimed at performance and inference efficiency, and it trained the 2B and 9B models with knowledge distillation. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
