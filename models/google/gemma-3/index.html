Gemma 3

Available · Language, Multimodal · Milestone

Gemma 3 is a family of open-weight models from Google, released on 12 March 2025 in 1B, 4B, 12B and 27B sizes, with a 270M size added in August 2025. Google says it is built from the same research and technology as Gemini 2.0; the 4B, 12B and 27B sizes accept images as well as text and have a 128K-token context window.
