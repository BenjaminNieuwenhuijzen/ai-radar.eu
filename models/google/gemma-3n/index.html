Gemma 3n

Available · Language, Multimodal

Gemma 3n is a family of open-weight models from Google for on-device use, previewed on 20 May 2025 and fully released on 26 June 2025 in E2B and E4B sizes. Google presents it as a mobile-first architecture developed with Qualcomm, MediaTek and Samsung System LSI; it accepts text, image, audio and video input and returns text.
