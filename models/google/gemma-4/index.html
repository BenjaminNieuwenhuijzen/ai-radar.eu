Gemma 4

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Gemma 4 is a family of open-weight models from Google, released on 2 April 2026 under the Apache 2.0 licence in E2B, E4B, 26B Mixture-of-Experts and 31B dense sizes, with a 12B Unified size added in June 2026. All sizes take text, image and video input and return text. Google says Gemma 4 is built from the same research and technology as Gemini 3. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
