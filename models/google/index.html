Google models

Google, headquartered in Mountain View, California, makes hundreds of products, among them Google Search, YouTube, Android and Gmail. Its AI research unit, Google DeepMind, was formed in 2023 by combining Google Brain and DeepMind. Google's language models include LaMDA, PaLM and the Gemini line, and since 2024 it has also released open-weights models under the Gemma name.
