LaMDA 2

Status unknown · Language · Milestone

LaMDA 2 is the second version of Google's conversational language model, announced at Google I/O in May 2022. Google offered it only through AI Test Kitchen, an app of experimental demos that began rolling out gradually to small groups of users in the US in August 2022. [1] [2] ● Primary source
