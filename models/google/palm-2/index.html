PaLM 2

Retired · Language · Milestone

PaLM 2 is Google's second-generation PaLM language model, announced at Google I/O in May 2023 in four sizes named Gecko, Otter, Bison and Unicorn, and offered the same day in Bard, through the PaLM API and in Vertex AI. Google withheld its parameter counts; on Vertex AI the text-bison and chat-bison models were retired in April 2025. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] ● Primary source
