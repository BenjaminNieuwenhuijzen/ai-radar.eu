Idefics2

Available · Language, Multimodal · Milestone

Idefics2 is an 8B multimodal model from Hugging Face that takes interleaved text and images and generates text, published as open weights under Apache 2.0 in base, instruction-tuned and chatty checkpoints. It is built on Mistral-7B-v0.1 and SigLIP, handles images at native resolution up to 980 x 980 pixels, and Hugging Face says it has enhanced OCR compared with Idefics1.
