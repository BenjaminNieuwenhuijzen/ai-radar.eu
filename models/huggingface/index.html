Hugging Face models

Hugging Face is a company founded in 2016, incorporated in the US as Hugging Face, Inc., with its main EU establishment in Paris. It runs a platform for hosting and sharing machine learning models and datasets, offers courses and tools, and says it works to democratise AI and machine learning. It also releases open models, including the SmolLM and SmolVLM families.
