SmolLM

Available · Language · Milestone

SmolLM is a family of small open-weight language models from Hugging Face in 135M, 360M and 1.7B sizes, each released as a base and an instruction-tuned checkpoint under Apache 2.0. Hugging Face trained them on its SmolLM-Corpus and presents them as small enough to run locally on devices from smartphones to laptops. [1] [2] ● Primary source
