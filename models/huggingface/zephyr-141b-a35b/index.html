Zephyr 141B-A35B

Available · Language

Zephyr 141B-A35B is a Zephyr chat model from Hugging Face, fine-tuned from the Mixture of Experts model Mixtral 8x22B v0.1 and published as open weights under Apache 2.0. The card says Argilla, KAIST and Hugging Face trained it together to test ORPO at a larger scale; it gives 141B total and 39B active parameters, corrected from the 35B in the name.
