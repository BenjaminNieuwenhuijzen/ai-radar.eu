Meta models

Meta Platforms, Inc., named Facebook, Inc. until October 2021, runs apps including Facebook, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp. Its FAIR research team dates from late 2013. Meta says it has released its Llama foundation models openly and expects to train both open and closed models; Meta Superintelligence Labs builds the Muse series of large language models. Known as Facebook, Inc. until 28 October 2021.

      • One of Meta's first Llama models built on mixture of experts: 128 routed experts plus a shared expert in alternating dense and MoE layers, 17B of 400B parameters active.
