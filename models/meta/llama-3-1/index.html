Llama 3.1

Available · Language

Llama 3.1 is a collection of Meta language models in 8B, 70B and 405B sizes, released on 23 July 2024 as open weights under the custom Llama 3.1 Community License and through more than 25 partners. It upgraded the Meta Llama 3 8B and 70B models, added a 405B model and raised the context length to 128K tokens, with support for eight languages. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
