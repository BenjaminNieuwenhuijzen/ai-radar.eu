Llama 4 Behemoth

Announced · Language · Milestone

Llama 4 Behemoth is a mixture-of-experts model in Meta's Llama 4 line, previewed in April 2025 while still in training, which Meta said it was not yet releasing. Meta describes it as multimodal, with 16 experts and 288B of nearly two trillion parameters active, and used it as the teacher model for distilling Llama 4 Scout and Llama 4 Maverick.
