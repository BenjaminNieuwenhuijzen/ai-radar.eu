Meta Llama 3

Available · Language · Milestone

Meta Llama 3 is the third generation of Meta's Llama language models, released on 18 April 2024 as open weights in 8B and 70B sizes, each pretrained and instruction-tuned, under the custom Llama 3 Community License. Meta also offered it through cloud partners and said its Meta AI assistant was built with Llama 3 technology. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
