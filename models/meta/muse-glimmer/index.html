Muse Glimmer

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Muse Glimmer is a 30-billion-parameter open-weights model in Meta's Muse series, released on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 licence for self-hosting. It accepts text and images and reasons before answering; Meta says it was distilled from Muse Spark outputs and aims it at local agent workflows on a Mac or PC with a single consumer GPU.
