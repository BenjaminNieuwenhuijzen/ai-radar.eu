OPT

Available · Language · Milestone

OPT (Open Pre-trained Transformers) is a suite of decoder-only language models from Meta, ranging from 125 million to 175 billion parameters, released on 3 May 2022 together with the metaseq code used to train them. The sizes up to 66B were released for download under a non-commercial licence; OPT-175B went to approved researchers on request. [1] [2] [3]README: Pretrained Model Weights ● Primary source
