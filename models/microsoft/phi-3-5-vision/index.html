Phi-3.5-vision

Available · Language, Multimodal

Phi-3.5-vision is a 4.2 billion parameter multimodal model from Microsoft that takes text and images and returns text, announced on 22 August 2024 with Phi-3.5-mini and Phi-3.5-MoE. It adds multi-frame input for comparing images and summarising image sets and video clips, and its weights are on Hugging Face under the MIT License. [1] [2] ● Primary source
