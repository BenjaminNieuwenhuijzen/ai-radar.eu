Phi-3.5

Available · Language

Phi-3.5 is a Microsoft release of two open-weight text models announced on 22 August 2024: Phi-3.5-mini (3.8 billion parameters) and the mixture-of-experts Phi-3.5-MoE (42 billion in total, 6.6 billion active). Both support multiple languages and 128K tokens of context; Phi-3.5-mini was also offered on a serverless endpoint in Azure AI Studio. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
