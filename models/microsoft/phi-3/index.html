Phi-3

Available · Language · Milestone

Phi-3 is a family of instruction-tuned small language models from Microsoft in three sizes: Phi-3-mini (3.8 billion parameters), released on 23 April 2024, and Phi-3-small (7 billion) and Phi-3-medium (14 billion), released at Microsoft Build on 21 May 2024. Each size offers up to 128K tokens of context; weights are on Hugging Face, and mini and medium were also hosted in Azure. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
