Phi-4-mini-reasoning

Available · Language, Reasoning

Phi-4-mini-reasoning is a 3.8-billion-parameter open-weight model from Microsoft for step-by-step math reasoning, released on 30 April 2025 on Azure AI Foundry and Hugging Face under the MIT licence. Built on Phi-4-mini with a 128K-token context, it is aimed at settings with limited compute or latency, such as education, embedded tutoring and edge or mobile devices. [1] [2] ● Primary source
