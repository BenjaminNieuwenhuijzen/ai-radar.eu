Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B is a 15-billion-parameter open-weight multimodal reasoning model from Microsoft Research, released on 4 March 2026 under the MIT licence through Hugging Face, GitHub and Microsoft Foundry. It feeds images into the Phi-4-reasoning language backbone through a SigLIP-2 vision encoder and can either reason step by step or answer directly. [1] [2] ● Primary source
