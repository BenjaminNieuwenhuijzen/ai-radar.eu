Phi-4-reasoning

Available · Language, Reasoning

Phi-4-reasoning is a 14-billion-parameter open-weight reasoning model from Microsoft, created by supervised fine-tuning of Phi-4 and released on 30 April 2025 on Azure AI Foundry and Hugging Face under the MIT licence. Its answers contain a chain-of-thought section followed by a summary; the Phi-4-reasoning-plus variant adds a short reinforcement learning phase. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
