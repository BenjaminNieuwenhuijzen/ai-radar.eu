Mistral AI models

Mistral AI is a Paris-based AI company that says it was founded in April 2023. It develops open-weight and commercial language models, as well as models for code, OCR, speech, embeddings and moderation, and offers enterprises and governments AI products including developer tools, applications and compute. Its first model, Mistral 7B, was released in September 2023 under the Apache 2.0 licence.
