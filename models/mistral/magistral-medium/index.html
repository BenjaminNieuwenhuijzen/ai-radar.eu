Magistral Medium

Retired · Language, Reasoning · Milestone

Magistral Medium is the enterprise variant of Magistral, which Mistral AI calls its first reasoning model, trained with reinforcement learning on top of Mistral Medium 3. It launched as a preview in Le Chat and via API on La Plateforme and Amazon SageMaker; Mistral AI highlights reasoning in the user's language with a traceable thought process.
