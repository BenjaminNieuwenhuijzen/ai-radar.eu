Magistral Small

Available · Language, Reasoning

Magistral Small is the 24B-parameter open-weights variant of Magistral, which Mistral AI calls its first reasoning model, released under Apache 2.0 and also offered as an API model. Its model card says it builds on Mistral Small 3.1, with supervised fine-tuning on Magistral Medium reasoning traces followed by reinforcement learning. [1] [2] [3]June 10, 2025 ● Primary source
