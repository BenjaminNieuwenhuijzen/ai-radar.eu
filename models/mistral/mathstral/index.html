Mathstral

Available · Language

Mathstral is a 7B language model from Mistral AI, built on Mistral 7B and specialised in mathematics and STEM subjects, aimed at problems that need complex, multi-step logical reasoning. Mistral released it as open weights under Apache 2.0, as part of its collaboration with Project Numina. [1] [2] ● Primary source
