Ministral

Available · Language

Les Ministraux are two language models, Ministral 3B and Ministral 8B, that Mistral AI introduced for on-device and edge use on the first anniversary of Mistral 7B, both with a context length of up to 128k tokens. Both were offered on the API, and weights for Ministral 8B Instruct were published for research use under the Mistral Research License. [1] [2] ● Primary source
