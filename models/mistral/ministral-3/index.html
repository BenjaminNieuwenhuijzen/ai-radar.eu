Ministral 3

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Ministral 3 is a series of dense Mistral AI language models in 3B, 8B and 14B sizes aimed at edge and local use, released on 2 December 2025 with Mistral Large 3. Each size has base, instruct and reasoning checkpoints with image input under Apache 2.0; Mistral's paper says all were derived from Mistral Small 3.1 by pruning and distillation.
