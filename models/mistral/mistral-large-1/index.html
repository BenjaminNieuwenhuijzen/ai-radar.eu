Mistral Large

Retired · Language · Milestone

Mistral Large, later named Mistral Large 1.0, is a language model that Mistral AI called its new flagship when it was released on 26 February 2024 on la Plateforme, on Microsoft Azure and in the le Chat beta. Mistral AI listed a 32K-token context window, native fluency in English, French, Spanish, German and Italian, and native function calling. [1] [2] ● Primary source
