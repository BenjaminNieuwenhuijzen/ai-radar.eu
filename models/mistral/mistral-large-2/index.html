Mistral Large 2

Available · Language · Milestone

Mistral Large 2 is the second generation of Mistral AI's Mistral Large language model, with 123 billion parameters and a 128k-token context window. It was offered on la Plateforme as mistral-large-2407, in le Chat and through cloud providers, and its instruct weights were published under the Mistral Research License for research and non-commercial use.
