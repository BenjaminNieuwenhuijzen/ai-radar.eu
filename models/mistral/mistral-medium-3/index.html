Mistral Medium 3

Retired · Language, Multimodal · Milestone

Mistral Medium 3 is a commercial multimodal model from Mistral AI with text and image input, offered through its API, on Amazon SageMaker and as the model behind Le Chat Enterprise. Mistral AI presents it for enterprise use, with hybrid, on-premises or in-VPC deployment and custom post-training. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
