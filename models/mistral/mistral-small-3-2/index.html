Mistral Small 3.2

Available · Language, Multimodal

Mistral Small 3.2 is a minor update of Mistral Small 3.1 Instruct, a 24B-parameter model with text and image input, published as open weights under Apache 2.0 and offered as API model mistral-small-2506. Mistral AI says it follows precise instructions better, repeats itself less and has a more robust function-calling template. [1] [2] [3]June 23, 2025 ● Primary source
