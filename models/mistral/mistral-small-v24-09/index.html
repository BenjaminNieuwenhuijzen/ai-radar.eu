Mistral Small v24.09

Available · Language

Mistral Small v24.09 is a 22-billion-parameter language model from Mistral AI, an upgrade of Mistral Small v24.02 that Mistral positions between Mistral NeMo and Mistral Large 2. It was offered on the API as mistral-small-2409, with weights under the Mistral Research License for non-commercial self-deployment. [1] [2]September 17, 2024 [3] ● Primary source
