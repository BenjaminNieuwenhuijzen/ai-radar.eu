Mixtral 8x7B

Available · Language · Milestone

Mixtral 8x7B is a sparse mixture-of-experts language model from Mistral AI with 46.7B parameters, 12.9B of them used per token, released on 11 December 2023 with open weights under Apache 2.0 and an instruct version. It was also served through the mistral-small API endpoint, later renamed open-mixtral-8x7b. [1] [2]February 26, 2024 [3] ● Primary source
