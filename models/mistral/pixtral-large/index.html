Pixtral Large

Available · Language, Multimodal

Pixtral Large is a 124B multimodal model from Mistral AI that takes text and images and outputs text, built on Mistral Large 2 with a 123B decoder and a 1B-parameter vision encoder. Mistral calls it the second model in its multimodal family; it was offered in le Chat, on the API and as weights under the Mistral Research License, with a commercial licence for commercial use.
