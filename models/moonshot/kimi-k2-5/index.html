Kimi K2.5

Available · Multimodal, Agentic, Code, Reasoning · Milestone

Kimi K2.5 is an open-weight native multimodal agentic model released on 2026-01-27. Moonshot AI built it by continued pre-training of Kimi K2 on about 15 trillion mixed visual and text tokens, and introduced an Agent Swarm mode in which the model coordinates sub-agents in parallel.
