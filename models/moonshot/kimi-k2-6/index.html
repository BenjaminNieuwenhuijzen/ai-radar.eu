Kimi K2.6

Available · Multimodal, Agentic, Code

Kimi K2.6 is an open-weight native multimodal agentic model released on 2026-04-20, with 1 trillion total and 32 billion activated parameters. Moonshot AI positions it for long-horizon coding and says its Agent Swarm scales to 300 sub-agents and 4,000 coordinated steps. [1] [2] [3] ◌ Evidence incomplete
