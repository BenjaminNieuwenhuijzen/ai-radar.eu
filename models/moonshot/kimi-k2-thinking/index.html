Kimi K2 Thinking

Available · Language, Reasoning, Agentic

Kimi K2 Thinking is an open-weight thinking-agent version of Kimi K2, released on 2025-11-06. Moonshot AI says it reasons step by step while using tools, keeps working across 200 to 300 sequential tool calls, has a 256K context window and ships with native INT4 quantisation.
