Kimi K3

Available · Multimodal, Agentic, Code, Reasoning · Milestone

Kimi K3 is a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with native vision and a 1-million-token context window, launched on 2026-07-16 in Kimi and the Kimi API, with weights following on 2026-07-27. Moonshot AI says it is built on a new architecture of Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals. [1] [2] ● Primary source
