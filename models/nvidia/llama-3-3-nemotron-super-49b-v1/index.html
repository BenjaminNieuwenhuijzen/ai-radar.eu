Llama-3.3-Nemotron-Super-49B-v1

Available · Language, Reasoning · Milestone

Llama-3.3-Nemotron-Super-49B-v1 is an open-weights reasoning language model from NVIDIA, released at GTC 2025 as the Super size of the Llama Nemotron family and also offered as a hosted API. NVIDIA reduced Meta's Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct to 49B parameters with neural architecture search and distillation, targeting a single H100 GPU; reasoning is switched on or off in the system prompt.
