Nemotron-3 8B

Available · Language · Milestone

Nemotron-3 8B is a family of 8-billion-parameter language models from NVIDIA, introduced on 15 November 2023 with its AI foundry service on Microsoft Azure: a base model, three chat models aligned with different methods and a question-answering model. The weights were offered through Hugging Face, the NGC catalog and the Azure AI model catalog under an NVIDIA community licence. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
