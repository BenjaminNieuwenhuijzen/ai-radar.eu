Nemotron 3 Ultra

Available · Language, Reasoning · Milestone

Nemotron 3 Ultra is an open-weights reasoning language model and the Ultra size of NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 family, with 550B total and 55B active parameters and a context length of up to 1M tokens. NVIDIA built it for long-running agents with contributions from the Nemotron Coalition and offered it as a download, on build.nvidia.com and through partners.
