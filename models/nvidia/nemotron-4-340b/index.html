Nemotron-4 340B

Available · Language

Nemotron-4 340B is a family of language models from NVIDIA (base, instruct and reward), released on 14 June 2024 as open weights on Hugging Face and the NGC catalog under the NVIDIA Open Model License. NVIDIA presented it for generating synthetic data to train other language models and sized it to fit on one eight-GPU DGX H100 in FP8 precision. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
