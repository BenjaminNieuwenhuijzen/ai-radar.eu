Nemotron-4 4B Instruct

Available · Language

Nemotron-4 4B Instruct is a small language model from NVIDIA, announced on 20 August 2024 for NVIDIA ACE, its game-character technology, as an NVIDIA NIM for cloud and on-device use. NVIDIA says it was distilled from Nemotron-4 15B and tuned for role-play, retrieval-augmented generation and function calling; weights followed on Hugging Face as Nemotron-Mini-4B-Instruct.
