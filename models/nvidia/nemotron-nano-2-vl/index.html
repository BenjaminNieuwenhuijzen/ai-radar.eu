Nemotron Nano 2 VL

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Nemotron Nano 2 VL is a 12B open-weights vision-language model from NVIDIA that takes text, images and video and produces text, built on the Nemotron Nano 2 12B reasoning model with a RADIO vision encoder. NVIDIA presents it for document, multi-image and video understanding; it introduced Efficient Video Sampling and was offered on Hugging Face, build.nvidia.com and as an NVIDIA NIM.
