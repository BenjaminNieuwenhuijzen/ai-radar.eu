ChatGPT

Status unknown · Language · Milestone

ChatGPT is a conversational language model from OpenAI, fine-tuned from a model in the GPT-3.5 series and offered from 30 November 2022 as a free research preview. OpenAI calls it a sibling model to InstructGPT and says it can answer follow-up questions, admit its mistakes, challenge incorrect premises and reject inappropriate requests. [1] ● Primary source
