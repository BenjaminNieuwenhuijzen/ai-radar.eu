GPT-4.1

Available · Language, Multimodal

GPT-4.1 is a non-reasoning language model in OpenAI's GPT line for text and image input, launched in the API on 14 April 2025 with GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano. OpenAI says the series improves on GPT-4o especially in coding and instruction following, with context windows of up to one million tokens. [1] [2] ● Primary source
