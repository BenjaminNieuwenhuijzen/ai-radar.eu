GPT-4 Turbo

Deprecated · Language, Multimodal

GPT-4 Turbo is an OpenAI model for the API, announced at DevDay on 6 November 2023 as a preview of the next generation of GPT-4 and offered that day as gpt-4-1106-preview. It has a 128K context window and lower prices than GPT-4; image input moved into the main model at general availability on 9 April 2024. [1] [2] ● Primary source
