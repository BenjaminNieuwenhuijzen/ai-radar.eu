GPT-5.3 Instant

Retired · Language, Multimodal

GPT-5.3 Instant is an update to the Instant model in OpenAI's ChatGPT for text and image input, released on 3 March 2026 to all ChatGPT users and in the API as gpt-5.3-chat-latest. OpenAI says it refuses less often without need and adds fewer defensive preambles. No GPT-5.3 Thinking model was released. [1] [2]Model details [3]Introduction ● Primary source
