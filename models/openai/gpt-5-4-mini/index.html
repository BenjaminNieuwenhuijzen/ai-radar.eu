GPT-5.4 mini

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

GPT-5.4 mini is a small OpenAI model for text and image input, released on 17 March 2026 with GPT-5.4 nano for high-volume workloads, in the API, Codex and ChatGPT. OpenAI says it brings many strengths of GPT-5.4 to a faster model. GPT-5.4 nano was offered only in the API and was deprecated on 1 October 2026.
