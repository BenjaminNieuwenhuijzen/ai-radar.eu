GPT-5.6 Sol

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

GPT-5.6 Sol is the flagship of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family, a reasoning language model for text and image input offered in ChatGPT, Codex, the API and Amazon Bedrock. Previewed with trusted partners on 26 June 2026 and released on 9 July 2026, it came with the new Sol, Terra and Luna tier names, Sol taking the place of the unsuffixed GPT-5 tier.
