GPT-6 Sol

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

GPT-6 Sol is the Sol tier of OpenAI's GPT-6 family, a reasoning language model for text and image input that OpenAI describes as a lower-cost alternative to GPT-6 Astra, trained with similar methods. It was released with GPT-6 Luna on 22 September 2026 in the API, Amazon Bedrock, ChatGPT Work and Codex, but not in ChatGPT conversations. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] ● Primary source
