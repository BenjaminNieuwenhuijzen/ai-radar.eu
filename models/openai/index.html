OpenAI models

OpenAI was announced in December 2015 as a non-profit artificial intelligence research company and states its mission as ensuring that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. It develops the GPT language models, the o-series reasoning models and the open-weight gpt-oss models, and offers its models through ChatGPT and the OpenAI API.
