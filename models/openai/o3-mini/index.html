o3-mini

Deprecated · Language, Reasoning

o3-mini is a small reasoning model in OpenAI's o-series for text input, previewed with o3 in December 2024 and released on 31 January 2025 in ChatGPT and the API. OpenAI calls it its first small reasoning model with function calling, Structured Outputs and developer messages, and it offers low, medium or high reasoning effort. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
