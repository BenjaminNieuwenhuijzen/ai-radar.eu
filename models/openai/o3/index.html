o3

Deprecated · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

o3 is a reasoning model in OpenAI's o-series for text and image input, previewed in December 2024 and released with o4-mini on 16 April 2025 in ChatGPT and the API. OpenAI says o3 and o4-mini were its first reasoning models able to use and combine every ChatGPT tool on their own, and that they can work with images within their chain of thought.
