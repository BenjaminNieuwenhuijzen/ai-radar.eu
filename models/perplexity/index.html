Perplexity models

Perplexity offers an answer engine that gives cited answers, Perplexity Computer for multi-step research and workflow tasks, the Comet AI browser and an API platform for developers. It describes routing work across models from several providers and calls Sonar its in-house model, optimised for answer quality in its default search mode.
