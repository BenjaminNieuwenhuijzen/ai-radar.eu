Llama 3.1 Sonar

Retired · Language

Llama 3.1 Sonar covers Perplexity API language models launched in July 2024: llama-3.1-sonar-small-128k and llama-3.1-sonar-large-128k as online (search) and chat ids, joined by August 2024 by an online-only huge model listed at 405B parameters. The online ids were removed on 22 February 2025 in favour of Sonar and Sonar Pro. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
