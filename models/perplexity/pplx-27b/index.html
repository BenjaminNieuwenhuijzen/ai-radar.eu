PPLX 27B

Available · Language

PPLX 27B is a language model that Perplexity post-trained from Qwen 3.8 27B, introduced on 25 August 2026 with Portable Computer, a version of Perplexity Computer that runs entirely on a local NVIDIA DGX Spark. It is available on the device to Pro and Max subscribers, and Perplexity trained it on synthetic tasks for its local Computer harness. [1] [2] ● Primary source
