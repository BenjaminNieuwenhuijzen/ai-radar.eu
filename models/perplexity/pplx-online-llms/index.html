PPLX Online LLMs

Retired · Language · Milestone

PPLX Online LLMs were two Perplexity language models, pplx-7b-online and pplx-70b-online, released on 29 November 2023 through pplx-api and the Perplexity Labs playground. Perplexity says they were fine-tuned from mistral-7b and llama2-70b to answer with website excerpts from its own search index; both had left the API by 14 May 2024. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
