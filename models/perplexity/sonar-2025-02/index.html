Sonar (February 2025)

Deprecated · Language

The February 2025 version of Sonar is Perplexity's in-house model built on Llama 3.3 70B, released under the unchanged name Sonar for Perplexity Pro users in the app and also usable through the Sonar API. Perplexity says it was further trained for answer factuality and readability in its default search mode; in the app it runs on Cerebras inference infrastructure. [1] ● Primary source
