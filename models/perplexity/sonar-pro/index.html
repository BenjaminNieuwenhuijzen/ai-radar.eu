Sonar Pro

Deprecated · Language, Multimodal

Sonar Pro is the higher tier of Perplexity's Sonar API, made generally available to developers on 21 January 2025 alongside Sonar. Perplexity positions it for enterprises with in-depth, multi-step queries; it has a 200k context window, accepted image input from April 2025, and Sonar Chat Completions support ended on 27 September 2026.
