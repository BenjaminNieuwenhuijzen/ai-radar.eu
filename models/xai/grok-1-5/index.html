Grok-1.5

Status unknown · Language

Grok-1.5 is a language model from xAI, announced on 28 March 2024 for early testers and existing Grok users on X. xAI says it improves reasoning and problem solving over Grok-1, most notably in coding and maths, and it raises the context window to 128,000 tokens. [1] ● Primary source
