Grok-1

Available · Language · Milestone

Grok-1 is the language model xAI developed for its Grok assistant, announced on 3 November 2023 and first offered as limited early access to users in the United States. It is a 314-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model, and on 17 March 2024 xAI released its base model weights under the Apache 2.0 licence. [1] [2] ● Primary source
