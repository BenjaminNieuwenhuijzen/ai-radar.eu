Grok-2 (December 2024 revision)

Status unknown · Language

The December 2024 revision of Grok-2 is a new version of that xAI language model, released under the same name and rolled out to all X users free of charge from 12 December 2024. xAI also added it to its API as grok-2-1212 and grok-2-vision-1212, and says it is three times faster, with better accuracy, instruction following and multilingual capabilities.
