Grok-2

Available · Language, Multimodal · Milestone

Grok-2 is a language model from xAI with text and vision understanding, released on 13 August 2024 as an early beta on X for Premium and Premium+ subscribers, together with Grok-2 mini, with an enterprise API announced for later that month. In August 2025 xAI published weights it calls Grok 2 on Hugging Face under the Grok 2 Community License Agreement. [1] [2] ● Primary source
