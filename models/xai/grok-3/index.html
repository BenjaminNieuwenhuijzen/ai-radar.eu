Grok 3

Retired · Language, Reasoning · Milestone

Grok 3 is a language model from xAI with a reasoning mode, Grok 3 (Think). It launched as a beta on 19 February 2025 for X Premium and Premium+ users on X and grok.com, became generally available on the xAI API on 3 April 2025 and was retired from the API on 15 May 2026. xAI says it was trained on its Colossus supercluster.
