Grok 4.1

Status unknown · Language, Reasoning

Grok 4.1 is an xAI language model released on 17 November 2025 to all users of grok.com, X and the iOS and Android apps, in Thinking and Non-Thinking configurations. xAI calls it an update to Grok 4 and Grok 3, with post-training aimed at style, personality, helpfulness and fewer factual hallucinations.
