Grok 4.3

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning

Grok 4.3 is a language model in xAI's Grok line that takes text and images and returns text, rolled out as a beta in the Grok apps on 17 April 2026 and also offered on the xAI API and on Amazon Bedrock. xAI describes it as a newly pre-trained model at the scale of Grok 4.20 with an improved architecture.
