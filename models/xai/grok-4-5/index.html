Grok 4.5

Available · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning, Code

Grok 4.5 is a language model in xAI's Grok line for text and image input, launched on 16 July 2026 in xAI's Grok Build coding agent, in Cursor and on the xAI API, and from 22 July in the Grok apps and on X. xAI says it was trained alongside Cursor and positions it for coding, agentic tasks and knowledge work.
