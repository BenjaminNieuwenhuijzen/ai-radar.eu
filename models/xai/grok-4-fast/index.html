Grok 4 Fast

Retired · Language, Reasoning

Grok 4 Fast is an xAI reasoning model that xAI describes as cost-efficient, released on 19 September 2025 in the Grok apps, including for free users, and on the xAI API with a 2-million-token context window. One set of weights serves both reasoning and non-reasoning responses, offered on the API as two model ids; both were retired on 15 May 2026. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
