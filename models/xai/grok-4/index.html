Grok 4

Retired · Language, Multimodal, Reasoning · Milestone

Grok 4 is an xAI reasoning model with text and image input, released on 9 July 2025 to SuperGrok and Premium+ subscribers and on the xAI API with a 256,000-token context window. xAI says it trained the model with reinforcement learning at pretraining scale and to use tools natively; a Grok 4 Heavy version came with a new subscription tier. It was retired from the API on 15 May 2026. [1] [2] [3] ● Primary source
