xAI models

xAI, which also does business as SpaceXAI, is the developer of the Grok AI models and assistant. Announced in July 2023, it says its mission is to accelerate human scientific discovery and that it builds AI to understand the universe. It operates a GPU supercomputer named Colossus. In February 2026 it reported that SpaceX had acquired the company.
